## 2. Exact residual exclusions: orbits 30 and 31

Canonical marked matrix:

    (1,1,2)
    (1,1,2)
    (2,2,5)

- **Orbit 30**: cyclic prefix row=0, cyclic suffix column=0, multiplicity 36. Row 2 and column 2 are nine-vertex *trees* whose three groups have sizes (2,2,5) and require two full collectors in the middle two-vertex group, (0,2,0).
- **Orbit 31**: cyclic prefix row=2, cyclic suffix column=2, multiplicity 9. Row 2 and column 2 are nine-vertex *unicyclic components*, group sizes (2,2,5), with all four vertices of the two small cells required as collectors, (2,2,0).

The standalone [exact local verifier](exclude_n17_orbits30_31.py) is **not** a relaxed oracle. It enumerates exact interleavable side-tree contact histories, preserving their ordered distinct contacts and induced knowledge states.

**Tree normal form.** In a connected component using m-1 calls, the underlying graph is a tree. Immediately before its final call, there are two disconnected contact components. Any complete collector at the end must be an endpoint of that final call. If two prescribed vertices are the complete collectors, the final contact is precisely between them. After removing it, each collector must already know all secrets in its respective connected side. Thus all possible local histories can be enumerated as two independent side trees, each ending with its collector pair's internal edge, followed by the final crossing call.

**Four-collector unicyclic normal form.** In a connected component using m calls, immediately before the last two calls there are at least two contact components. If there are three components, the first of the last two calls cannot communicate all m secrets and after the final call at most its two endpoints can be fully informed. If there are two components but fewer than four distinct endpoints participating in the last two calls, at most three vertices can become full. Therefore **four specified final collectors require exactly two initial components and two cross-component calls using four distinct endpoints**. Each of the four endpoints must already know every secret on its own side before the two crossing calls; consequently both prior components are trees, each with two collector endpoints whose last internal call joins them. The verifier considers all valid partitions, all side-tree contact chronologies, and both crossing matchings.

The remaining five-vertex group must intersect both sides: otherwise its members cannot collectively know all m secrets after the final crossings, which exclusively involve the four specified collectors. The verifier additionally imposes mandatory full collective coverage in every cell. It computes the exact B bitmask domains (which within-cell subsets collectively know the whole component) and D domains (all source-secret receiving-subsets within each cell). A necessary connection between a prefix component and a suffix component requires **B & D == D** cellwise.
