# Primary prior-art anchors

These are starting points for verification, not an exhaustive priority certificate. Visit the original sources and verify exact hypotheses before applying results.

1. **Arnaud Casteigts**, *Open questions in temporal graphs*, research tracker, updated September 22, 2026 — https://arnaudcasteigts.net/blog/open-problems.html. Tracks the `2n-3` temporal clique conjecture.
2. **Daniele Carnevale, Arnaud Casteigts, Timothée Corsini**, *Dismountability in Temporal Cliques Revisited*, SAND 2025 — https://doi.org/10.4230/LIPIcs.SAND.2025.6. Pivotability, 2-arch/2-degenerate temporal structures, and constructions.
3. **Júlia Baligács**, *Temporal Cliques Admit Linear Spanners* (2026) — https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.05156. Universal linear-size temporal-clique sparsification.
4. **Júlia Baligács and collaborators**, *Almost Linear 3-Spanners of Temporal Cliques* (2026) — https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02851. This is about **temporal stretch** and must **not** be confused with static treewidth 3.

Research statements about gossip-theoretic lower bounds, the `2n-4` limit, and classical minimum gossip configurations require exact citation verification before a manuscript is drafted. Bibliographic metadata or open-question status may change after the snapshot date.
